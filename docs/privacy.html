Privacy Policy

Ploop · Effective 27 November 2026

Ploop is a flight log that keeps your flights on your phone. The app is free and shows a small number of ads, served by Google AdMob. This policy explains what stays on your device, what the ad service collects, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your flights, your name and everything else you enter stay on your device. Ploop has no accounts or servers, and never sends your flights to us or to advertisers.
  • Ploop shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google collects some device data, such as your advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads (details below).
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time.
  • Ploop works offline. Ads simply don't appear when you're offline.

What Ploop stores on your device

Ploop keeps the following in a private database on your phone, which other apps can't read:

  • Flights you add: date, departure and arrival airports, airline, flight number, seat, cabin, departure and arrival times, aircraft, registration and your notes.
  • Settings: your name for the passport (optional), home airport, distance unit, theme, Ploop mode, the date you first opened the app, and whether you found a hidden easter egg.

This data never leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself (see below).

Boarding pass scanning

  • Camera. When you scan a boarding pass, the camera image is processed on your device to read the barcode. No photo or video is saved.
  • Screenshots. If you pick a screenshot instead, Ploop reads only the image you choose, once, to find the barcode. It doesn't keep a copy.
  • What we keep from the barcode. Only the flight details listed above. Your name, booking reference (PNR), check-in sequence number and the raw barcode contents are read past and never stored.
  • Barcode reading uses Google's ML Kit library, which runs on your device. The camera image and barcode contents are not sent anywhere. ML Kit may send Google anonymous metrics about how the scanner performs, under Google's ML Kit terms.

When data leaves your device (only when you choose)

  • Saving images. Story and passport images are saved to a "Ploop" album in your photo library.
  • Sharing. When you tap Share, Android's share sheet opens and you decide which app receives the image. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Backups. Export creates a JSON file with your flights and settings, saved wherever you choose. Import reads a backup file you pick.

Story images can show your flights, routes, dates and the name you set. You can hide exact dates before sharing. The barcode on a flight story encodes only the route and distance (for example "Flew 3,284 km, CMB to DXB").

Advertising

Ploop shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The ads appear as cards marked "Ad" inside the app. When the app is online, the SDK contacts Google to load ads and may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers: your Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Network and approximate location: your IP address, which can indicate your rough location (country or city).
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, language, app version.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and diagnostic information about the ads SDK.

Google uses this data to serve, measure and improve ads, to prevent fraud and, where you've allowed it, to personalise the ads you see. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What Ploop never gives the ads service: your flights, airports, dates, notes, passport name or anything else you enter. Ploop doesn't use your flights to pick or target ads.

Your choices:

  • Consent. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other places where it's required, Google's consent form appears the first time you open Ploop. You can choose whether ads may be personalised. To change your answer later, use Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Offline. Ploop works fully offline. Ads loaded while you were online may still show for up to an hour; after that, no ads are shown until you're online again. Ads are kept only in memory, never saved to your device's storage.

Other third parties

Ploop contains no analytics or tracking software beyond the ads SDK described above. Google Play may collect information about app installs and updates under Google's own privacy policy; Ploop has no access to that data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it there. You can delete any flight in the app, or delete everything by clearing Ploop's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. We can't recover or delete your flights for you, because we never have a copy. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted under Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and your phone's ad settings.

Children

Ploop is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app is not intended to show ads to children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
